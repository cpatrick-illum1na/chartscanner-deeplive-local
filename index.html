About this DeepLive replica

This dashboard searches Yahoo Finance for common stocks listed on the NYSE or Nasdaq and cryptocurrencies. The exchange restriction applies only to stocks; crypto remains available. Selected charts download intraday OHLCV bars through the local Node server. The provider may delay, throttle, or change access. QQQ is retained in the starter watchlist as a Nasdaq-listed ETF, not a common stock. The Parliament-style scores are deterministic local indicator calculations (EMA, RSI, momentum and range position). An optional LM Studio local model can provide a separate interpretation. No broker is connected.
